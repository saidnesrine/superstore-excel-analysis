# Superstore-Excel-Analysis

This project analyzes the Global Superstore 2016 dataset using Microsoft Excel to explore sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and product performance.
The project includes data cleaning, PivotTable analysis, KPI calculations, and three interactive dashboards designed to support data-driven business decisions.

## Dataset
The dataset used in this project is the Global Superstore 2016 dataset, which contains transactional sales data including: <br>
 - Order information <br>
 - Customer segments <br>
 - Product categories and sub-categories <br>
 - Sales, profit, and discounts <br>
 - Geographic markets and regions <br>
 
The dataset includes over 50,000 orders across multiple global markets.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit performance across regions and markets.<br>
- Identify the most profitable product categories and sub-categories.<br>
- Compare customer segments based on sales, profit, and profit margin.<br>
- Identify the top-performing customers and products.<br>
- Investigate the relationship between discount levels and profitability.<br>

## Data Cleaning
Before performing the analysis, several data preparation steps were applied:
Removed duplicate records <br>
Standardized text fields using TRIM and CLEAN <br>
Converted date fields into proper Excel date format <br>
Created derived columns: <br>
-Order Year <br>
-Order Month <br>
-Shipping Delay <br>
-Profit Margin <br>
Verified numeric fields such as Sales, Profit, and Discount <br>
Performed data quality checks <br>

## Dashboard
1. Global Overview Dashboard
Provides an overview of sales, profit, profit margin, order count, monthly sales trends, regional performance, market profitability, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

2. Product Analysis Dashboard
Analyzes sales and profit by product category, highlights the top 10 products by profit, and compares profit margins across sub-categories.

3. Customer Analysis Dashboard
Compares customer segments by sales and profit, examines profit margins, and identifies the top 10 customers by profit.

## Key Business Insights
- Profitability varies across regions and markets. The analysis highlights differences in regional performance and opportunities to investigate underperforming areas.<br>
- Higher discount levels are associated with lower profitability, and some heavily discounted transactions generate negative profits.<br>
- The Consumer segment shows the highest profit among the customer segments analyzed, making it an important segment to monitor and develop.<br>
- Technology is the most profitable product category, highlighting its contribution to overall business profitability.<br>
- Tables sub-category generates negative profit despite its sales activity, suggesting a need to investigate pricing, discounting, and cost factors.<br>
- A relatively small group of customers and products contributes substantially to business performance, making customer profitability and product contribution analysis valuable for prioritizing commercial efforts.<br>

## Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can improve business performance: 
- Review high-discount transactions and establish discount thresholds that protect profitability while remaining competitive.
- Focus sales and marketing efforts on products and categories that contribute strongly to total profit, while monitoring their sales performance.
- Investigate the Tables sub-category and other loss-making products to identify whether pricing, discounts, shipping costs, or other factors explain their negative profitability.
- Strengthen relationships with profitable Consumer customers and identify opportunities to improve customer retention and long-term value.
- Investigate underperforming regions and markets to identify opportunities to improve margins, pricing, and operational efficiency.
- Track sales, total profit, profit margin, and discount levels regularly to evaluate the impact of business decisions.
